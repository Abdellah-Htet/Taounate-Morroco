About Taounate

Taounate Province

Introduction

Taounate (تاونات) (ⵜⴰⵡⵏⴰⵜ) is a city located in northern Morocco, about 80 km from Fès. It is known for its mountains, natural landscapes, and water resources. It became the capital of the Taounate Province in 1977 and belongs to the Fès-Meknès region.

Culture and Society

Taounate is part of the Jbala region, a mountainous area in northern Morocco. The Jbala people are known as mountain inhabitants. Their dialect is influenced by the Idrissid era and reflects a rich cultural heritage dating back to the 11th century.

Traditional Crafts

  • Weaving in Oulad Azam
  • Basket weaving in Moulay Bouchta El Khammar
  • Woodwork in Bni Oulid
  • Pottery in Aslas

Hydrological Resources

  • Idriss 1st Dam (34°07′N, 4°40′O)
  • Oued Asra River
  • Al Wahda Dam
  • Asfalou Dam
